## What the data is, and is not

Each file is a dated copy of an official register as its authority published it, or a record of what changed between two such copies. A listing is not advice, not a recommendation, and not a judgement about any institution. Where an authority corrects its own record, the correction is kept alongside the original rather than replacing it.

The underlying registers remain the property of their publishers, whose own terms are recorded per source in the README. Canadian institution names are not republished here: IRCC reserves republication rights over the designated learning institutions list, so this repository holds dated counts and change events for Canada rather than names.
